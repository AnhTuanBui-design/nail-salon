Not sure what to book?

Quick answers.

Manicures

Gel or regular polish?

Regular polish is quick and easy to change. Gel lasts two to three weeks and stays shiny, but needs a soak-off removal.

 Enhancements

Fill or new full set?

Book a fill if your set is two to three weeks old and intact. If several nails have lifted, or you want a new shape, book a full set.

 Enhancements

Acrylic, Gel-X or builder gel?

Acrylic is the strongest for long shapes. Gel-X feels lighter and more natural. Builder gel strengthens without adding length.

Acrylic, Gel-X or dip: which one is right for you?

Strength, feel, upkeep and price, compared side by side.

4 min readNovember 2026By the Pedi N Nails team
Article photo

Enhancements add strength, length or both. Acrylic, Gel-X and dip powder can all look beautiful; they just feel different and need different upkeep.

Acrylic

Acrylic is a powder and liquid mixed into a bead and sculpted onto the nail or a tip. It's the strongest option and holds long, dramatic shapes like coffin and stiletto well. Expect a fill every two to three weeks.

Gel-X

Gel-X uses full-cover soft gel tips that are bonded to your nail and cured under a lamp. Sets are thin, light and natural-looking, and they soak off more gently than acrylic. They're a good fit for short to medium length.

Dip powder

Dip powder builds colour and strength in layers without a UV lamp. It's harder-wearing than gel polish and great on natural nails that bend or peel, but it isn't meant for much added length.

Still not sure?

Tell us how you use your hands and how long you want your nails, and your technician will recommend one. If you're coming in with a set from another salon, add a removal so we have enough time.

Fill or full set?

Book a fill if your set is two to three weeks old and intact. If several nails have lifted, book a new full set.

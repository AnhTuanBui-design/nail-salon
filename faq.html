FAQ & policies

Before you book.

Answers to the questions we hear most. Still not sure? Call us at (416) 555-0142.

Booking

Do I need an appointment?

Booking online is the surest way to get the time and technician you want. Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free; call ahead to check same-day availability.

How do I book?

Use the Book button on any page, or call us. You'll get a confirmation by text and email, with a link to change or cancel.

Can I choose my technician?

Yes. Pick a technician when you book, or choose No preference and we'll match you with the first available.

Do you take group bookings?

Yes, for bridal parties, birthdays and friends. For three or more people, please call so we can seat you together.

Services

Should I book a fill or a new full set?

Book a fill if your set is two to three weeks old and still intact. If more than two nails have lifted or broken, or you want a new shape or length, book a full set.

What's the difference between gel and regular polish?

Regular polish air-dries and is easy to change at home. Gel is cured under a lamp, stays glossy for two to three weeks and needs a soak-off removal.

Can you copy a design from a photo?

Usually, yes. Bring the photo or add it to your booking notes. We'll tell you the price and time before we start.

Do you do kids' nails?

Yes. Kids under 10 can have a manicure, pedicure or polish change. A parent or guardian should stay in the salon.

Pricing & payment

Do nail designs cost extra?

Yes. Simple art starts at $5 for two nails and custom sets start at $20. Your technician will confirm the price first.

Why do some prices say “from”?

Enhancements and nail art depend on length and detail. The listed price is the starting point; your technician confirms before starting.

How can I pay?

Payment methods are to be confirmed. Tips can be added on card.

Policies

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel or move your appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before, using the link in your confirmation.

What if I'm running late?

Please call us. If you're more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten the service or move your appointment.

Do you need a deposit?

Not for regular appointments. Groups of three or more may be asked for a deposit to hold the chairs.

What if my nails chip?

If gel chips or lifts within 7 days, come back and we'll fix that nail free of charge.

Aftercare

How do I make my gel last longer?

Wear gloves for cleaning and dishes, use cuticle oil daily, and don't pick or peel. Book a removal instead of pulling gel off.

How often should I get a fill?

Every two to three weeks for acrylic and Gel-X. Waiting longer makes lifting and breaks more likely.

Is it safe to remove gel at home?

It's safer to book a removal. Peeling gel takes layers of your natural nail with it.

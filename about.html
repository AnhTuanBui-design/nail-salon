Our story

Pedi N Nails Howard opened in [year] with a simple idea: a nail appointment should feel like time off, not another errand. We kept the room quiet, the chairs comfortable and the schedule loose enough that nobody feels rushed.

Our technicians have been doing nails for between six and twelve years. Some love long sculpted sets, some love hand-painted art, and one of them is famous for his pedicure massage. You can book any of them, or let us match you.

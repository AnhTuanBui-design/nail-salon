Questions

Before you book.

Can't find your answer? Call us at (416) 555-0142.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking online is the surest way to get the time and technician you want. Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free; call ahead to check same-day availability.

Do nail designs cost extra?

Yes. Simple art starts at $5 for two nails and custom sets start at $20. Bring a photo and your technician will give you a price before starting.

Should I book a fill or a new full set?

Book a fill if your enhancements are two to three weeks old and still intact. If more than two nails have lifted or broken, or you want a new shape or length, book a full set.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment, using the link in your confirmation. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may mean rescheduling.

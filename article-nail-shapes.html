Almond, square, coffin: finding your nail shape

What each shape suits, and which ones hold up best for everyday life.

4 min readNovember 2026By the Pedi N Nails team
Article photo

Colour gets the attention, but shape is what people notice first. It changes how long your fingers look and how well your nails hold up day to day.

Everyday shapes

  • Square has a straight tip and sharp corners. It looks clean and modern on short nails and suits French tips well.
  • Squoval is square with softened corners. It's the most practical shape and the least likely to snag.
  • Oval follows the curve of your cuticle. It makes fingers look longer and works on any length.

Statement shapes

  • Almond tapers to a soft point. It's elegant and lengthening, and holds up best with Gel-X or acrylic.
  • Coffin is long and tapered with a flat tip. It shows off ombré and art, but needs the strength of an enhancement.
  • Stiletto comes to a sharp point. It's the boldest shape and best left to acrylic.

How to choose

If you type all day or work with your hands, keep to squoval or short oval. If you want length, almond is the most forgiving. Not sure? Ask your technician to shape one nail first so you can see it before committing.

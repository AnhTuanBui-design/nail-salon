Gel or regular polish? How to choose in two minutes

How long each lasts, how removal works, and which one suits your week.

3 min readNovember 2026By the Pedi N Nails team
Article photo

It's the question we hear most at the front desk. Both are good choices. The right one depends on how long you want your colour to last, and how often you like to change it.

Regular polish

Regular polish is painted on and air-dries. It's the quickest option, it costs less, and you can take it off at home with remover whenever you want a new colour.

The trade-off is wear. Most people see small chips within four to seven days, especially if they type a lot or wash dishes by hand.

Gel polish

Gel is painted on in thin layers and cured under an LED lamp after each coat. It's dry the moment you leave, so there's no waiting or smudging, and it keeps its shine for two to three weeks.

Gel needs a proper soak-off to remove. Peeling it off yourself pulls away layers of your natural nail, so book a gel removal when you're ready for a change.

Which one should you book?

  • Book regular polish if you like changing colour every week, or you want a quick, low-cost tidy-up.
  • Book gel for holidays, events, or any time you don't want to think about your nails for a couple of weeks.
  • Book gel on your toes if you're heading somewhere warm. A gel pedicure easily lasts a month.
